Frequently asked questions

How can I book?

Online, choosing the scan, day and time from the free slots, or by phone on +39 0932 954441 or +39 351 374 6102. If you are not sure which scan to book, call us.

How do I move or cancel an appointment?

Let me know in good time on +39 351 374 6102: we will move the appointment or, if you paid online, refund you.

Is the practice open on Saturdays?

No. Patients are seen by appointment from Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 12:30 and 15:00 to 19:00.

Where can I park?

On the streets near the practice. If you don't know the area, call us: we'll guide you there and tell you where to park. More directions are on the practice page.
