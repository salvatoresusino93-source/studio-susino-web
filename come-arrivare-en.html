How to get here

Via dell'Arno 34, Pozzallo (RG), Italy, at Arcobaleno Dentisti.

The address

Via dell'Arno, 34 — 97016 Pozzallo (RG), Italy
at Arcobaleno Dentisti, right in the centre of Pozzallo.

Get directions Open in Google Maps

The entrance

The ultrasound practice is inside Arcobaleno Dentisti: use the dental practice entrance at number 34. Come in and ask at reception for Dr. Salvatore Susino's ultrasound practice.

Where are you coming from?

Tap a link to open the route in Google Maps, already set from your starting point.

  • From Ispica: follow the signs for Pozzallo and drive into the town centre; about 15 minutes by car. Route from Ispica.
  • From Modica: head down towards the coast in the direction of Pozzallo and continue to the centre; about 25 minutes by car. Route from Modica.
  • From Scicli: along the coast via Sampieri, or inland; about 30 minutes by car. Route from Scicli.
  • From the port of Pozzallo (ferries from Malta): the port is south of the town; the practice is in the centre, a few minutes by car or taxi, about half an hour on foot. Route from the port.
  • From Pozzallo railway station (regional trains): the station is close to the centre; 10–15 minutes on foot. Route from the station.

Parking and access

Parking is on the streets near the practice: there is no reserved car park.

If walking is difficult for you or you use a wheelchair, call us before your appointment: we will tell you what the access is like and how we can help.

By public transport

Pozzallo is connected to the nearby towns by regional trains and local buses. Check the timetables before you leave and, if you are coming by public transport, tell us when you book: we will choose a convenient time together.

Can't find the way?

Call +39 0932 954441 or +39 351 374 6102: we will guide you to the entrance. Opening hours, how your visit works and what to bring are on the practice page.
