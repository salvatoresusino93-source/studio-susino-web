Frequently asked questions

How much does the scan cost?

€80, the same for every scan. The written report, the images and the explanation at the end are included. See the fee page.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

For a scan at the practice a doctor's referral is not compulsory, but it is advisable: it is always better to have a scan after your doctor has assessed you. If you have one, bring it with you along with any previous scans: it helps guide the ultrasound.

Do I need to fast?

Yes, for 6–8 hours, for an upper or complete abdominal scan, a kidney scan and a Doppler of the abdominal aorta or renal arteries. You may drink water and take your usual medication. Fasting is not needed for the other scans.

Do I need a full bladder?

Yes, for a lower abdominal scan (the lower part of the abdomen), a complete abdominal scan, a urinary tract scan and a bladder and prostate scan: finish drinking about a litre of water an hour before and do not urinate after that. For the complete abdomen fasting is also needed. It is not needed for the other scans.

How long does the exam take?

It depends on the scan: usually between 10 and 30 minutes, and you will find the typical duration on each scan’s page. At the end I give you the report and explain in plain words what was found.

Are there any contraindications? Can it be done during pregnancy?

Ultrasound has no known contraindications: it uses sound waves, not X-rays. It can also be done during pregnancy and repeated whenever your doctor considers it useful.

Can I go back to my usual activities after the scan?

Yes, straight away, including driving: the gel is wiped off and no recovery time is needed. If you were fasting, you can eat as soon as it is over.
