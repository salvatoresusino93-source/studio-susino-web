Ultrasound scans at home

If you cannot come to the practice, the scan can be done at home. The appointment is arranged by phone.

Who a home ultrasound scan is for

A home ultrasound scan is carried out at the patient's home. The service is meant for people who find it hard to travel: older people, bedridden patients, people who are convalescing. The scans are performed by Dr. Salvatore Susino, medical specialist in Radiology.

You can ask for the same scans that are carried out at the practice. Whether a scan can be done at home is assessed during the phone call.

Home scans are also available at care homes and other facilities, by arrangement.

Where home scans are available

In Pozzallo, the province of Ragusa and part of the province of Syracuse, within about 50 km: for example Ispica, Modica, Scicli, Ragusa, Rosolini and Pachino. Whether your area can be reached is checked on the phone, depending on time and availability.

How the appointment is arranged

A home scan cannot be booked online: it is arranged by phone. During the call we agree on the day and time, depending on your area and availability, and we ask which scan your doctor has requested, to understand whether it can be done at home.

To arrange a scan at home we ask for the doctor's referral: it helps us understand what is needed and whether it can be done at home. Please also bring any previous test results, if you have them.

Report

The report is handed to you at the end of the scan, at home too.
