Newborn hip ultrasound in Pozzallo

Newborn hip ultrasound performed personally by the radiologist, by appointment, at the practice in Pozzallo (RG), Italy. Report and explanation at the end of the scan.

What it is for

Ultrasound screening of the hips in newborns and infants (Graf method) to rule out dislocation or developmental hip dysplasia. Indicated in the first weeks of life, in the presence of risk factors (breech birth, family history, oligohydramnios) or suspicious clinical findings; often repeated at follow-up.

Why it is done

Done in the first weeks of life to rule out a hip that has not formed well or that comes out of place, especially if there are risk factors or the paediatrician requests it.

How it works

The baby stays lying down or in the parent's arms. A small probe is passed over the hips, with gel.

What I check

We check whether the baby's hip is mature and stable, using the method used in paediatrics (Graf).

Is any preparation needed?

No. This scan needs no fasting and no preparation. If you have previous reports or scans, bring them with you: they help to compare and follow any change over time.

How much it costs

A single fee of €80 for every exam: includes the scan, the written report handed to you straight away and a spoken explanation. See fees

Frequently asked questions

Does the newborn hip ultrasound hurt?

No, it is painless. The probe is placed on the skin with a little gel. It uses ultrasound, not radiation: it is safe and can be repeated as often as needed, including during pregnancy and in children.

Is any preparation needed?

No. This scan needs no fasting and no preparation. If you have previous reports or scans, bring them with you: they help to compare and follow any change over time.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

For a private appointment it is not compulsory. If you have a referral, bring it along with any previous scans: it helps to focus the examination on the right clinical question.

How long does it take and when do I get the report?

The scan usually takes a few minutes. At the end I hand you the written report and explain in plain words what was found and whether further checks are needed.
