Data handled through this website

This information website has no forms and does not directly collect health data. The server and technical services may process connection and security data such as IP address, date and time, requested page, browser and errors. If you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, we process the information you provide in order to reply. Please avoid sending unnecessary clinical details through these channels.

Purposes and legal bases

  • replying to requests and arranging appointments, to take steps requested before providing a service;
  • complying with applicable legal obligations.

Recipients, transfers and retention

Technical data may be handled by hosting, network and analytics providers acting as processors where required. Email and WhatsApp are separate services; when used, data also passes through their providers. Some providers may process data outside the EEA using safeguards required by the GDPR.

Data is kept only as long as needed for these purposes and, for communications with administrative or clinical relevance, for periods required by law. Technical log retention follows the providers’ retention policies.
