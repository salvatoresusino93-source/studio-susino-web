Urinary tract ultrasound in Pozzallo

Urinary tract ultrasound performed personally by the radiologist, by appointment, at the practice in Pozzallo (RG), Italy. Report and explanation at the end of the scan.

What it is for

Assesses the kidneys, ureters, bladder and post-void residual urine. Prescribed for renal colic, blood in the urine, recurrent urinary infections, suspected obstruction of the urinary tract or monitoring of known kidney disease.

Why it is done

You have it if you have renal colic, blood in the urine, frequent urinary infections, or your doctor suspects an obstruction to the flow of urine.

How it works

First we look with a full bladder; then, if needed, we ask you to urinate and check how much urine remains in the bladder. Gel on the abdomen or flank.

What I check

We assess the kidneys, ureters and bladder, and measure any urine left after voiding.

Is any preparation needed?

Yes: you need to arrive with a full bladder. Drink about a litre of water an hour before and do not go to the toilet until the scan: a full bladder acts as a window onto the pelvic organs.

How much it costs

A single fee of €80 for every exam: includes the scan, the written report handed to you straight away and a spoken explanation. See fees

Frequently asked questions

Does the urinary tract ultrasound hurt?

No, it is painless. The probe is placed on the skin with a little gel. It uses ultrasound, not radiation: it is safe and can be repeated as often as needed, including during pregnancy and in children.

Is any preparation needed?

Yes: you need to arrive with a full bladder. Drink about a litre of water an hour before and do not go to the toilet until the scan: a full bladder acts as a window onto the pelvic organs.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

For a private appointment it is not compulsory. If you have a referral, bring it along with any previous scans: it helps to focus the examination on the right clinical question.

How long does it take and when do I get the report?

The scan usually takes a few minutes. At the end I hand you the written report and explain in plain words what was found and whether further checks are needed.
