Ultrasound scans and exams

The exams I perform at the practice, grouped by area. Each one has a short explanation; tap "Learn more" to find out why it is done, how it is carried out and what we check. Can't find the one your doctor requested? Call us.

Abdomen

  • Complete abdominal ultrasound

    A check of the abdomen: liver, gallbladder, pancreas, spleen, kidneys, bladder and other organs.

    Learn more →

  • Upper abdominal ultrasound

    A check of the upper abdomen: liver, gallbladder, pancreas, spleen and kidneys.

    Learn more →

  • Lower abdominal ultrasound

    A check of the lower abdomen: kidneys, bladder, prostate and pelvic organs.

    Learn more →

Urinary tract and urology

  • Urinary tract ultrasound

    A check of the kidneys, urinary tract and bladder, including after urinating.

    Learn more →

  • Kidney ultrasound

    A check of the kidneys and the first part of the urinary tract.

    Learn more →

  • Bladder and prostate ultrasound

    A check of the bladder and prostate from above the pubic bone.

    Learn more →

  • Scrotal and testicular ultrasound

    A check of the testicles, epididymis and scrotal structures.

    Learn more →

Thyroid and neck

  • Thyroid ultrasound

    A check of the thyroid in the neck: size, shape and any nodules.

    Learn more →

  • Neck ultrasound

    A check of the thyroid, salivary glands, lymph nodes and other neck structures.

    Learn more →

Musculoskeletal

  • Musculoskeletal ultrasound

    A check of muscles, tendons and ligaments in the area that hurts.

    Learn more →

  • Shoulder ultrasound

    A check of the tendons and structures of the shoulder.

    Learn more →

  • Knee ultrasound

    A check of the tendons, ligaments and any fluid in the knee.

    Learn more →

  • Hip ultrasound

    A check of the tendons and soft-tissue structures around the hip.

    Learn more →

  • Elbow ultrasound

    A check of the tendons and bursae of the elbow.

    Learn more →

  • Wrist and hand ultrasound

    A check of the tendons, nerves and joints of the wrist and hand.

    Learn more →

  • Ankle and foot ultrasound

    A check of the ligaments, Achilles tendon and structures of the foot.

    Learn more →

  • Soft-tissue ultrasound

    A check of a swelling or lump under the skin.

    Learn more →

Paediatric

  • Newborn hip ultrasound

    A check of the hips of a newborn or young infant.

    Learn more →

Vascular (Doppler)

  • Carotid (supra-aortic) Doppler ultrasound

    A check of the blood flow in the neck arteries (carotids).

    Learn more →

  • Abdominal aorta Doppler ultrasound

    A check of the abdominal aorta and the blood flow.

    Learn more →

  • Renal artery Doppler ultrasound

    A check of the flow in the arteries that supply the kidneys.

    Learn more →

  • Lower-limb Doppler ultrasound

    A check of the arteries and/or veins of the legs and feet.

    Learn more →

  • Upper-limb Doppler ultrasound

    A check of the arteries and veins of the arms and forearms.

    Learn more →

Other

  • Lymph node ultrasound

    A check of enlarged lymph nodes in the neck, armpits or groin.
